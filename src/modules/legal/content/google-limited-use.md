# Google API Services User Data Policy Disclosure

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

This disclosure outlines Subwise’s strict compliance with Google's Limited Use requirements under the Google API Services User Data Policy.

---

> **Mandatory Policy Compliance**:
> Subwise’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## 1. Strictly Prohibited Uses

Subwise adheres to the following non-negotiable consumer protection standards:

- **No Advertising**: We do not use or transfer your Google user data for serving ads, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
- **No AI Model Training**: Google user data is never used or transferred to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
- **No Sale or Brokerage**: We never sell or transfer your email data or personal identifiers to data brokers, advertising networks, or third parties.

## 2. Minimal & Narrow Scope

We exclusively request the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly` scope to perform search queries for transactional subscription receipts.

- Access is restricted exclusively to user-facing features prominently visible in your Subwise dashboard.
- Personal correspondence, chats, social emails, and non-transactional messages are completely ignored.

## 3. Human Access Restrictions

Humans are prohibited from reading your email messages. In rare technical support investigations, access would only occur if you provide prior affirmative consent for a specific message, or where required by law.

## 4. User Control & Revocation

You can revoke Subwise's access at any moment with one click in Subwise Profile Settings or through [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
